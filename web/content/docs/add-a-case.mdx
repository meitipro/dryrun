<Callout type="warn">Commit to a prediction before the run. A case is a test: you say what you expect, and the rules answer.</Callout>

## The command line

Open a suite and press **dryrun add**, or go to `/r/<id>/add`. The page is a command line:

1. Type the **scenario**, at most 240 characters. Write the situation as a sentence about what happened, not a question about the rules.
2. Pick the **expected** answer, `ALLOW` or `BLOCK`. These are the only two predictions an author can make. `UNCLEAR` is what the validators find, never what you predict.
3. Press **run it, 1 GEN**. The wallet signs two transactions: `add_case`, which holds your stake of exactly 1 GEN, and `run`, which sends the case to the validators.

If the second transaction fails, the case is already added. Anyone can run it from the suite page, and the stake and any bounty are still yours.

## The three outcomes

| Result | What the validators said | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `PASS` | the label is your expectation | the stake comes back to your balance |
| `FAIL` | the label is the opposite | the stake comes back, and the case is flagged for the owner |
| `UNCLEAR` | the rules do not settle it | the stake comes back, and you earn the bounty of 2 GEN from the pool while it has funds |

Nothing is paid out by the run. The stake and the bounty are added to your balance, and **withdraw** on [My rules and cases](/me) is the only call that sends money.

## Limits

- At most 40 **live** cases per rule set. The owner or the author can retire a case, which frees its slot. A retired case stays visible, with who retired it.
- A case runs once per version of the rules. After an update it shows `STALE` and can run again.
- A case you retire before it has run returns its stake and earns nothing.
